Backcourt Rally

You play from behind your own shoulder. When the CPU hits, a dashed arc shows the shuttle's path and a ring marks where it lands (red means it's going out, so leave it). The blue ring is where to stand, and the blue ball is where you'll make contact. Pick a shot any time. Your player swings on their own when the shuttle arrives, as long as you're in position.

W A S D
Move. Sprinting drains stamina.
Space
All high shots: clears from the back, lifts from the front, defensive lifts against smashes (also the high serve). Hold longer for more power: higher power is deeper and flatter.
K
All soft shots: drops from the back, net shots at the front, and blocks against smashes (also the short serve). At the net the green band on the meter is the sweet spot: a quick tap underhits into the tape, holding too long floats it up to be killed.
L
Smash. Jump smash on high contact if you have stamina.
Aim
← → (or Q E) pick the left or right line. Opposite your contact side means cross-court. A tap is remembered until your shot goes. The white cross shows the target.
Timing
Choose your shot while the blue contact ball glows gold for a Perfect: smashes have the widest window, net shots and blocks the tightest. Picking too early is less accurate.
Power
Hold longer for more power (and depth), release to lock it. Past the red line shots can go long.
Stamina
Running and smashing wear you down and only a little comes back between rallies. Tired players move slower and hit less accurately.
Pause
Esc or P to pause, restart or change mode.
Mouse works too: left button works like Space, right button drops. Serves must land in the diagonal box. The umpire sits on the high chair and the service judge sits on the low chair opposite.
